Explore LRSD's Multi-Year Strategic Plan in Action

A transparent view of progress, learning, and next steps across LRSD's four strategic priorities.

The Multi-Year Strategic Plan (MYSP) 2023-2027 is more than a strategic document. It is a living record of how Louis Riel School Division is working toward its vision for students, staff, families and communities. This site brings together the division's goals, progress, evidence and learning in one accessible place.

Visitors can explore the plan's four priorities: Belonging, Mastery, Independence and Generosity, and see how strategic actions are progressing over time. The site highlights what has changed since the plan was launched, what the division is learning, and where further work is needed. Rather than presenting a simple scorecard, it offers a transparent look at successes, challenges and ongoing growth.

  • Belonging
  • Mastery
  • Independence
  • Generosity

You'll also find links to the full strategic plan, previous plans, annual reports to the community, supporting resources, and opportunities to provide feedback. Whether you're a staff member, parent, student or community member, this site offers a clear view of how LRSD is turning its commitments into action and measuring progress along the way.
